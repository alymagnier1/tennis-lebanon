What’s next

More ways to enjoy your tennis.

We’re starting with helping Beirut players find each other. Here’s what we’re planning next.

  • Coach-verified levels

    An on-court Grip Test with a certified coach appointed by RacketBound, at a designated location.

  • Local leagues

    More opportunities to compete and play regularly with the Beirut community.

  • Find a coach

    Connect with coaches to work on your game.

These features are planned for later and aren’t included in initial access.
