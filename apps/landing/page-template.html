Good to know

Before you join

Who is this for?

Adults 18 and over, of all genders and levels, who can play tennis in Beirut. New to tennis or already playing regularly? You’re welcome. Choose the level that feels closest to your game.

Why start with 50 players in Beirut?

A nearby player at your level, free when you are, is what makes a match possible. Fifty is our first community target. Invitations go out as enough players with overlapping levels, courts and free time join, so the order you join in isn’t the only factor. If there aren’t enough players near your level yet, you stay on the list and we’ll invite you as soon as there are.

What happens after I join?

We’ll use your chosen contact to invite you to the app when there are enough players near your level and times. Joining here expresses your interest; it doesn’t create an app account or reserve a court.

Can I join if I live outside Beirut?

Yes, if you can regularly play in Beirut. If you mostly play elsewhere, join anyway and tell us where. It helps us decide where to open next.

Is the app available on iPhone and Android?

Android testing is underway. App Store and Google Play releases are planned. Your invitation will explain which access options are available; joining doesn’t guarantee an immediate download.

Does joining reserve a court?

No. RacketBound helps you find players and arrange a match. You still confirm availability, booking and court fees directly with the club.
