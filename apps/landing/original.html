How it works

From “I want to play” to on court.

  1. Find a player at your level

    See players near your level who are free when you are.

  2. Agree a time

    Suggest up to three times. Everyone in the match votes.

  3. Ask the club on WhatsApp, then play

    Book the court with the club, the way you do now. Add it to the match so everyone knows.

How it works. Three lines, not a feature grid (brand guide §8). Step 3 says “Ask the club on WhatsApp” because booking isn’t instant, so “Book now” would be a false promise.

Questions

Before you join

Will other players see my phone number?+

No. Players see your first name, level and area, never your number. We only use it to send your download link.

Why do you need my WhatsApp and email?+

WhatsApp is how we reach you when Beirut opens and help set up your first match. Email is how you sign in to the app, with a link instead of a password. We don’t share either with players or clubs.

Is my level an official ranking?+

No. You pick the line that sounds most like you, and your matches confirm it. Your level shows as provisional until you’ve played a few.

Does it cost anything?+

RacketBound is free during early access. You pay the club for the court, as you do today.

iPhone or Android?+

Both. You’ll get the right link for your phone when Beirut opens.

I don’t play in Beirut.+

We’re starting with one city so matches are easy to find. Join anyway and tell us where you play. We’ll message you when your area opens.

FAQ. Only answer “Both” once TestFlight works. Until then, change it to “Android first, iPhone soon” or hold the ads.
